1) Who we are

Atomic Diets (“we”, “us”, “our”) provides an AI-powered health and fitness subscription platform. Our service helps you generate personalized diet, workout, and weight-loss guidance based on the information you provide.

2) What information we collect

Depending on how you use the service, we may collect:

  • Account information (e.g., name and email) if you create/login via our authentication flow.
  • Health and fitness input (e.g., age, gender, height, weight, activity level, workout timing, diet type, and country).
  • Subscription and billing data processed by Stripe (we do not store full card details).
  • Usage data related to generating and viewing reports (e.g., reports you request and dashboard interactions).

3) How we use your information

  • To generate your personalized plan, including meal plans and workout schedules.
  • To provide your dashboard, streaks, progress tracking, and downloadable reports.
  • To manage subscriptions, billing, and account access.
  • To improve the service and our user experience.

4) AI and third-party services

We use third-party service providers to operate our platform and to generate reports. This may include: Firebase for authentication and storing user data, Stripe for billing/subscriptions, and Anthropic Claude API (AI) to generate personalized content.

When you submit your health form, we may send relevant data to the AI service so it can produce your plan.

5) Data storage and retention

We store user data to operate the service, including health reports and progress entries. Your subscription status may affect how long we retain certain data.

6) Your choices

  • You can review and update certain account details through your account access flow.
  • You can cancel your subscription via Stripe.
  • You can request deletion of your account data by contacting us.

7) Security

We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8) Children’s privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13. If we learn we have collected information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete it.

9) Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the service means you accept the updated policy.
